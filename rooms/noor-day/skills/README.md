## How it shaped the room

It warns that a cream background with a terracotta accent and identical rounded cards are the
commonest generated look, which was the first idea here. The board took its palette from the
subject instead (the vegetables on Esraa's list on a pale enamel fridge), spends its one bold
move on the water glass, and keeps everything else quiet.

## Considered and left out

- `web-artifacts-builder`: for standalone artifacts, not a room in this app.
